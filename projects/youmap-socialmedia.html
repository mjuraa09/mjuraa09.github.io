YouMap

YouMap is a real-time social mapping app that lets users create and share interactive maps centered around any topic — from travel and food to nightlife, events, sports, and local news. The core idea is "maps that tell stories," combining elements of social media with geolocation so people can map moments as they happen and connect with others over shared interests.
The platform works around three main actions: creating custom maps (with control over appearance and participation), posting location-based moments that follow each map's format, and connecting by following maps, joining conversations, and discovering what's happening nearby or globally. Maps can cover categories like travel, city life, food & drink, arts & culture, shopping, transportation, animals, and health & beauty.

Overview

Our Product Design, UX/UI, iOS Development, and Back-End teams worked synergistically to build an innovative solution.

The YouMap Story

We noticed that current mapping experiences lack customisation – users want to know about places that fit their personal preferences. YouMap fills this gap by providing a network of social-driven locations.  It allows users to customise their feed, create their own maps, and join communities interested in similar spots.

Wireframes

Based on the knowledge gained from product discovery workshops and market research, we started to design all the necessary app screens.

Low-fidelity screens

The aim of wireframes was to provide a visual understanding of a product in its early stage.

Wireframes allowed us to get stakeholder and project team approval before the creative phase  got under way.
We designed the app screens in their most basic form, without delving into more complex visual details, like shadows or illustrations.

This allowed us to build the core of the application  – information architecture and the flow of the most important functionalities.
Example of wireframes

Usability testing

To start the design phase, we wanted to verify our assumptions and test the solutions we’ve come up with during UX wireframes.  Our goal was to check the app’s intuitiveness, whether users understand the flows  and features. The tests consisted of a free exploration session and several tasks.

Summary & Metrics

YouMap didn't ship with live analytics during this engagement, so instead of usage data we tracked our own process: how the discovery findings, design iterations, and scope decisions moved the concept toward a buildable MVP.

Summary

Without live usage data to lean on, tracking discovery, design, and scope metrics kept the process accountable — every design decision could be traced back to a research finding or a stakeholder trade-off, not a guess.

Hand-over

The application was completed and handed over to an external team for further development. My goal, together with the team, was to create the first version and transform the client's idea into a concrete product

End-result

Testing revealed that the first iteration of the post creation feature was too complex, and in the next iteration, this feature was rebuilt. Ultimately, the client was satisfied with the results of our work, as they received an initial product with the required functionalities in place, ready for further development.
