My name is

Mateusz

Jurkiewicz

With 10 years of experience in UI/UX design, I specialize in crafting scalable interfaces and meaningful interactions across complex digital products.

I design user interfaces and microinteractions with a strong focus on usability and business impact, working closely with developers, stakeholders, and clients throughout the entire product lifecycle.

As part of a Design System team, I help build and maintain a large-scale design system adopted across 10+ projects. My responsibilities span defining governance and contribution models, managing design and development workflows in Figma and Storybook, and keeping documentation and changelogs up to date — ensuring consistency and efficiency at scale.

Alongside this, I lead efforts in a product redesign initiative, covering end-to-end activities: conducting user interviews, gathering business requirements, iterating on designs, and validating solutions through usability testing.
